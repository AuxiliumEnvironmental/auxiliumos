# Strategic rationale and build discipline
Assessment from the reviewed product plans and current owner direction, 2026-10-08. This is a reasoned business recommendation, not a measured competitor ranking or financial forecast.

Build the complete AuxiliumOS destination as the operating layer for the broader company while preserving Moldo as a focused independent operating application. The OS's strongest potential value is controlled, repeatable delivery across enterprise accounts, facilities and service lines: one effective scope/authority record, qualified review, reliable document releases, site memory, vendor coordination, traceable costs and actionable executive reporting. Moldo's strength is the focused daily mold-service experience. Those strengths fit together without forcing all employees into one interface or merging every database.

An OS feature earns its place when it reduces missed handoffs, repeated data entry, scope leakage, review bottlenecks, unbilled commitments or enterprise coordination work, or improves recurring client visibility. A dashboard alone does not create that advantage. The underlying records, SOP transitions, permissions and operating adoption must work. The company advantage would come from consistent execution and accumulated operational knowledge, not the number of screens or use of AI by itself.

Preserve all 20 modules while implementing dependencies in an order that avoids rework: source reconciliation and domain contracts; identity/tenant and document-version controls; complete ordinary engagement lifecycle; recurring enterprise/MSA/readiness lifecycle; vendor/finance/reporting; controlled integrations and runtime AI; measured reliability and recovery. Extend proven shared mechanisms rather than cloning workflows for every client. A modular architecture with explicit domain ownership is the initial engineering default; infrastructure separation should follow measured needs. No speculative microservices or orchestration platform is required just to run development agents.

Track observable business outcomes before and after adoption: request-to-reviewed-scope time, authorization delays, release rework, unapproved/unbilled change rate, project margin with attributable costs, overdue owner actions, repeat work using site history, and enterprise reporting preparation effort. Targets need real baselines; do not invent savings, competitive superiority or model delivery dates. Capacity and recurring-contract value depend on implementation quality, pricing, operations and customer use, not software completion alone.

Moldo sale optionality improves when contracts, billing ownership, IDs, permission boundaries and exports are explicit, and ordinary Moldo operation survives OS disconnection. Whether an LLC, holding company or later sale is appropriate also depends on actual contracts, IP, taxes and legal advice. Software architecture cannot settle those matters; OD-016 records the review boundary.

The largest demonstrated current risk is confusing detailed plans with implemented controls. The archive contains valuable plans and a small static/schema foundation. New continuation tooling improves execution discipline; it does not replace building and proving the actual OS. Full final acceptance is REQUIREMENTS.json, not this package or a model's confidence.
