## Versioned block and application contract

A limitation block has stable ID/version, triggering facts, service/intent/area applicability, source, proposed client summary, agreement text, report text, supersession/effective dates, required reviewer, and approval/activation status. The wording may differ by context while preserving consistent meaning. Link supporting record and the exact scope/recommendation/declination that caused application.

An agreement block also specifies required variables, compatible/incompatible blocks, commercial/legal reviewer, signer/approval dependencies and version. Missing rates, caps, payment terms, jurisdictional restrictions, approved insurance assumptions or retention obligations remain named configuration decisions. Do not substitute values from another job or Moldo without explicit applicability and authority.

Development default: assemble a clearly proposed package from selected scope and recorded decisions, surface contradictions, and route to review. Keep accepted, declined, deferred, excluded and inaccessible work distinct. A limitation cannot authorize forbidden work, erase an applicable duty, invent a scientific conclusion, or replace emergency/safety action. Selecting an add-on can remove a limitation only after the necessary scope, qualifications and authorization are satisfied.

Emergency authorization is limited to its recorded purpose and cap. The historical roadmap calls for follow-up authorization for sampling, reports, protocols, PRV, extended consulting and other services unless already included. Do not treat urgency or a broad MSA as blanket permission.

## Dependencies and acceptance

- Scope controls included/excluded work and areas; authorized amendments determine the effective revision. Communications can propose changes only.
- A repeated request or reused template does not copy stale acceptance, signatures, limitations or commercial approvals.
- Synthetic packages cover declined sampling, limited/inaccessible areas, an added service after approval, unsupported specialist work, emergency limitation, and contradictory block selections.
- Final documents show only the applicable, reviewed limitation versions. Agreements, reports and UI summaries remain semantically consistent.
- Preserve signed/released versions and decision history. New template wording never silently changes a signed agreement or released report.
- Build the assembly and review workflow now using synthetic facts and proposed text. Record every unresolved owner/professional choice and keep unapproved live terms inactive while other development continues.
