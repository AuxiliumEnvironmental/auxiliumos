Authority: [OWNER_DECISIONS.json](../../OWNER_DECISIONS.json), OD-001, OD-003, OD-006, OD-007, OD-011 and OD-016. Coverage/evidence: [REQUIREMENTS.json](../../REQUIREMENTS.json).

## Separate audiences

Auxilium leadership sees permitted operating performance across accounts and services. Client executives see only their assigned account/program/portfolio information and approved released executive content. An executive title does not grant technical approval, signing, finance or unrestricted document rights. Independent suspended/removed membership denial overrides other role assignments.

## Dashboard contracts

| View | Source and meaning |
|---|---|
| Work / Capacity | Requests, authorized Projects, assigned tasks and workload; distinguish work from waiting |
| Facilities / Readiness | Activated sites, current site passports, open recommendations, owners and due dates |
| Scope / Exposure | Pending authorizations, changing caps, blocked deliverables and recorded exceptions |
| Financial | Permitted operational costs/revenue/caps/reserves and export status; distinguish estimate, committed and actual |
| Service / QBR | Response and delivery measures with definition, period, denominator and source links |
| Moldo | Permitted branch summaries and linked engagements with source and synchronization timestamp |

Dashboards derive from source records. Unknown values remain unknown, not zero or green. Counts apply the viewer's access boundaries, including drilldowns and exports. Metrics display update time and any excluded/incomplete data. Readiness indicators describe recorded evidence, not a blanket safety/compliance certification.

## Actions and acceptance

Each exception links to an owner and operational record. Executive views may request follow-up but cannot bypass scope, scientific review or document release. QBR packages require controlled revision and approved audience; raw audit logs stay internal unless a separately filtered policy permits disclosure.

Test totals against known fixtures, restricted-record non-disclosure through aggregates/drilldowns, stale integration labeling, timezone/period boundaries and large portfolio pagination. Do not blend Moldo financial records twice when OS mirrors its summaries. Moldo-only management/staff gain no OS access; companion work is deferred. Refresh failures keep the last known timestamp visible and identify affected widgets rather than claiming current data.
