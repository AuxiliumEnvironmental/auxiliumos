# Vendor Portal Map

Status: Proposed final-system design under the 2026-10-08 development mandate; not implemented or finally policy-approved. Authority: [OWNER_DECISIONS.json](../../OWNER_DECISIONS.json), OD-003, OD-010, OD-012 and OD-017. Coverage/evidence: [REQUIREMENTS.json](../../REQUIREMENTS.json). Vendor delivery is sequenced after its dependencies, not removed from the full OS destination.

## Permitted surfaces

- Assigned Work: only assigned tasks/work orders, minimum facility/access instructions, due dates and approved materials needed to perform that work.
- Work Detail: instructions, acknowledged changes, status updates and questions linked to the assignment. A status update cannot expand authorized scope or price.
- Uploads / Closeout: credential documents, work evidence and closeout packages with upload status and review feedback.
- Company Profile / Credentials: vendor's own permitted contacts and credentials; expiration warnings do not imply professional validation.

No unrelated account/project browsing, other-vendor records, Auxilium internal notes, executive reports, private financial records or unrestricted document library. Billing visibility requires a specific approved vendor relationship and permission. Vendor uploads remain unverified supporting records until reviewed and are never automatically client-visible.

## Access and task lifecycle

Check active identity/membership, vendor relationship and assignment at the backend for list, detail, upload and download. Removing assignment or suspending membership denies future access regardless of another role or cached UI. Document grants remain narrower than work assignment; paths and possession of identifiers grant nothing.

Vendor acknowledgement is not client authorization. A requested change creates a reviewable proposal routed to Auxilium; accepted changes link to the approved scope/authorization. No PHI in documents, messages or fixtures. Professional conclusions remain assigned to qualified humans.

## Acceptance and recovery

Test another vendor's identifiers, revoked assignments, cross-account attachments, restricted-file direct links, duplicate upload retries and closeout rejection/resubmission. Preserve submitted versions and reviewer history. Failed uploads stay retryable; do not mark closeout complete before all required items persist and pass review. Notifications use approved recipients/channels and record delivery failures without repeating contractual commitments.

Moldo integration creates no automatic vendor access to OS or Moldo. Companion functionality is deferred. Pending real vendor authority/contact decisions gate only live onboarding or outbound actions while synthetic development continues.
